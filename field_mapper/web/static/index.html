    Calibrate the camera

    Calibration measures the lens so pixels can be turned into field positions. Do it once per camera, at the resolution you run at, with autofocus off.

    1. Print a checkerboard: open printable board. Glue it to something flat.
    2. Measure one square and check the numbers below.
    3. Press Start, then capture 15+ views covering the whole image.

    A board with 10 × 7 squares has 9 × 6 inner corners.

    Mapping pauses while calibrating.

    • Cover the corners and edges of the image, not just the middle.
    • Tilt the board different ways and vary the distance.
    • Hold still for each capture; avoid glare on the board.
